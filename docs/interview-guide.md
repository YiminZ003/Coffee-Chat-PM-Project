# Open Chair: user interviews (team deliverable)

**Required by the course:** at least two real interviews; four if you have time. Don't present fictional feedback as interview findings.

## Before the demo (open-ended discovery)

1. Think back to the last time you wanted career advice. What did you do?
2. How do you decide who to reach out to for a coffee chat?
3. What makes it hard or uncomfortable to start a networking conversation?
4. Can you walk me through a networking message you actually sent, if any?
5. How would you define a successful coffee chat?
6. What do you do when someone does not respond?

Avoid showing the product before these questions; don't lead the interviewee toward your features.

## Last part of interview — usability test

Give them the prototype link. Explain clearly that all listed people are fictional samples. Ask them to:

- Create or edit a profile.
- Find an alumnus in their target industry.
- Open a profile and generate a conversation starter.
- Explain which parts seemed useful, confusing, or unrealistic.

Ask what action they'd actually take next (spend five minutes, try a real connection, recommend to a classmate, etc.). Don't pressure them into commitments.
